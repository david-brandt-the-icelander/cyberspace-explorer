See how a repository is put together

Give the address of a GitHub or GitLab repository, or of a folder in one. The browser draws the diagrams, lists the behavior views and shows the knowledge files kept in its .dev/ folders, and you walk from one folder into the next by clicking the parts in the drawings.

    Or open retro-games as an example. The views follow the skills and the knowledge catalogue of human-assisted software development.

    A private repository needs you to sign in with GitHub, or an access token. Give its address and the browser will ask; a token pasted here stays in this browser and goes to that host alone.
